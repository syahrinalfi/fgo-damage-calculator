# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack

TypeScript end-to-end — data layer, simulation/optimizer engine, and a Vite+React web app. One language is used across the stack for native fetch/JSON handling, direct reuse of the engine in the browser, and operation without a separate backend service.

## Users

Two audiences sharing one engine:
- **Newer / low-roster players** stuck on content that feels "too hard" but is often clearable with what they already own, just not built or played optimally. Their question: "can I clear this at all, and how."
- **Experienced players** optimizing tight farming clears for 90++ (Lv.90+) events, raids, and other offensive-clear content where margins are tight and exact skill/card ordering matters. Their question: "what's the fastest/most reliable line."

## Product Purpose

A data-driven FGO (Fate/Grand Order) damage & NP calculator that evolves into a **fastest-clear farming optimizer** and a **strongest single-target (ST) burst finder**, backed by real Atlas Academy servant/CE/enemy data and the user's actual (possibly unfinished) account state — not hypothetical max-investment numbers.

The optimizer's target turn count is **not hardcoded to 3**. Clear Quest searches iteratively from one turn up to a bounded search horizon; 3T Farming is only a preset. The initial product horizon is at most 6 turns because longer offensive-only plans become increasingly misleading while enemy turns are not simulated. Max Burst is a separate product mode with a different objective: maximize damage against a chosen target, immediately or after a bounded number of setup turns.

Success means the user gets an actionable, per-wave/per-hit plan (servants, skills, card order, NP timing, Order Changes) they can execute in-game, or — when nothing clears yet — a concrete "what would need to change" gap analysis.

## Positioning

Simulates actual per-hit/per-card damage and NP-gauge mechanics from real Atlas Academy servant/CE data at the user's *actual* entered account state (real skill/NP/append levels, Fou values, bond, CE MLB status) — not a generic stat-based estimator and not assuming maxed investment. Initial quest input is manual and supports different class, attribute, HP, and optional traits/buffs for every enemy in every wave. The same simulation core serves Clear Quest, Max Burst, and the later Manual/Sandbox surface.

## Operating Context

- Primary data source: `https://api.atlasacademy.io` (`nice` endpoints), fetched at runtime/build time — never hand-curated servant/CE data, since it would rot on every game patch.
- Region is a user-switchable setting with **fully separate stored account data per region** (JP and NA/EN are independent accounts with independent roster state, not a merged dataset).
- User's roster (servant levels, skill levels, NP level, ascension/append skills, Fou values, bond level, equipped CE + level/MLB, Mystic Code) is entered and stored manually/locally per account — this is private data Atlas Academy doesn't have.
- CE inventory is a list of owned copies (not a boolean "own/don't own" set) — duplicates are common and the optimizer must respect actual copy counts.
- Initial quest setup is manual: the user chooses the wave count and defines each enemy independently. Class, attribute, and HP are required; traits and special buffs are optional advanced inputs. Omitted advanced values use explicitly disclosed neutral defaults.
- The optimizer may recommend one borrowed support. Its standard idealized build uses Atlas Academy's natural maximum level (no Grails), NP1, Fou +1000 ATK/HP, skills 10/10/10, append skills disabled, and every strengthening available in the selected region unlocked.
- The borrowed support never counts toward party Cost (confirmed by the project owner, 2026-11-08) and may share the field with the owned copy of the same servant (e.g. a second Altria Caster). Clear Quest offers a default, editable support list: Altria Caster, Koyanskaya of Light, Scáthach-Skadi, Oberon, Merlin and Zhuge Liang.
- Borrowed-support CE effects are deliberately ignored in the first milestone. The support starts at 0% NP and may use its NP only if the simulated line legitimately reaches 100%. A later opt-in may attach a specific CE; the optimizer must not silently assume a 50% starting-NP CE.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Confirmed in scope (see `research/00-project-overview.md` §1 for full detail):
- **Clear Quest / Minimum Turn** for manually entered nodes, free quests, raids, and event-farming quests. Search horizons expand from 1 through at most 6 turns; 3T is a preset, not a global default.
- Clear Quest prefers one necessary attacker, then progressively expands to two or more only when a reliable clear cannot be found. A user-pinned preferred attacker must be the primary damage dealer, not merely present in the party.
- 3T-style farming preferences: core/free-slot tagging, Total Cost reporting, Minimum-Cost mode, Prefer-Event-CE mode, Prefer-Bond-CE mode, Top-5 distinct strategies, and fallback suggestions. Event CEs are simulated as combat equipment too: starting NP, ATK, NP Damage, and other effects can make an Event CE part of the viable loop rather than merely a reward modifier.
- Strongest ST Burst Finder, including multi-turn setup strategies (not single-turn-only).
- Manual/Sandbox Builder reusing the same simulation core as the optimizers.
- "Not Clearable Yet" Gap Analysis / Build-Up Advisor (smallest concrete upgrade that flips fail→clear).

Initial ranking policy for Clear Quest is: full quest clear, minimum turns, acceptable reliability, fewer necessary attackers, safety margin, then the selected Event CE/Bond CE/Minimum Cost preference. Attacker count is causal: a servant is an attacker/core only when removing its damage contribution makes the line fail; incidental face-card damage does not automatically make a support an attacker.

Explicit non-goals for the first milestone: Challenge Quests; enemy attack/survivability simulation; borrowed-support CE search; gacha/rolling or investment-cost modeling; native/mobile app; and automatic quest selection. CQ and enemy turns are deferred because their enemy-AI/RNG and survivability requirements would make offensive-only recommendations misleading, not because Atlas Academy lacks the data.

Open, unsourced mechanics tracked as implementation-time follow-ups (not blockers): card-draw weighting/guarantee rule and evaded/invincible-blocked hit's gauge/star yield. Star carryover and Order Change state transfer were resolved during the research follow-ups; see `research/09-simulation-and-optimizer-design.md` §7 and the originating mechanic files.

Current implementation is an engine-first work in progress. The simulator advances waves, enforces legal NP gauge, preserves per-hit NP refund, supports ST/AoE target lists with per-enemy server modifiers, models NP as one of exactly three attack slots, and produces automatic Extra Attacks for Brave Chains. `optimizeQuest` resolves the legal `NiceNp` version from `selectedNpId` plus the account's unlocked `questId:phase` strengthening keys, then models `npDistribution`, functions before/after damage, support NPs, NP-Chain Overcharge, Ignore-Defense NP damage, ordinary trait special damage, active-state-trait damage, conjunctive-trait damage, servant-rarity damage, trait-count damage on either caster or target, and low-HP damage scaling. Trait-count damage includes static traits plus matching buff instances, honors `ParamAddMaxCount`, and preserves the two live records requiring unavailable unreleaseable-individuality metadata as fail-closed. Multi-enemy chains follow the confirmed target rule: selected target first; same-servant face cards may Overkill; servant/NP boundaries auto-switch by view priority `3 -> 2 -> 1`. `optimizeQuestMinimumTurns` searches horizons 1 through at most 6, uses bounded beam pruning, progressively widens from one damage dealer, attributes necessary attackers/core members by counterfactual replay, and returns at most five strategy-diverse results. An optional seeded finalist pass rebuilds the initial card bag/hand and replays the exact action log across 1–10,000 trials, resampling later draws, crit rolls, and damage variance; it reports clear count/probability plus a 95% Wilson interval and ranks reliability before attacker count. `findClearUpgradeRecommendations` tests isolated skill-level, CE current-cap level, NP-level, cumulative NP-strengthening, immediately feasible MLB+max-level, and owned-roster build scenarios through that same optimizer, returning only upgrades proven to flip fail→clear. Owned-roster scenarios may add an owned servant outside the shortlist or replace its underbuilt shortlist version; they raise it to at least natural max level, Ascension 4, Fou +1000/+1000, skills 10/10/10, and regional NP strengthenings while preserving the account's actual NP level, append skills, Grails, and investment above those baselines. They do not recommend unowned servant acquisition. CE ownership records use limit-break stages 0–4; MLB consumes only enough separate, unmerged owned copies and otherwise falls back to the current stage's Atlas-provided level cap. Equipped CEs select the highest eligible Atlas `condLimitCount` tier per skill slot before Turn 1; supported self buffs (including ATK/NP Damage), starting NP, and front-line opening-star effects feed the same battle state and therefore the Upgrade Advisor can prove passive-driven MLB clears. Versioned JSON repositories persist JP/NA roster profiles and manual quest drafts through a DOM-free `localStorage`-compatible contract; manual per-enemy inputs validate and convert to optimizer `Wave[]`, while `buildRosterOptimizerInputs` hydrates stored account builds with fetched Atlas servant/CE records, unlocked skill versions, and copy-aware CE state. `loadPersistedOptimizerSession` orchestrates one region's stored roster and quest drafts, deduplicates the necessary Atlas servant/CE fetches, and returns optimizer-ready inputs. The web first screen now has functional Clear Quest and Roster input stages with explicit Quest/Roster navigation, region-separated local persistence, Atlas-backed servant/CE/Mystic Code search, immutable roster editing, validation feedback, and responsive layouts; preferences, optimizer execution/results, and remaining modes remain later flow stages, and its current styling is functional direction rather than final visual approval. Field-dependent CE party auras and re-triggering entry effects after Order Change remain fail-closed until CE presence lifecycle is modeled. Product completion still requires the battle-point NP family and unreleaseable-individuality metadata, adaptive per-hand strategy re-search and unmodeled debuff RNG, preferences/results editor stages, Max Burst/Manual mode surfaces, and final visual approval.

**2026-09-21 amendment:** the web-state clause immediately above is superseded for Clear Quest. Persisted JP/NA inputs now drive a Preferences stage and a Results stage: the browser adapter loads the selected manual draft, hydrated roster, live battle constants, and the selected Master level's live `maxCost`, then runs the existing bounded `optimizeQuestMinimumTurns` flow with owned skill sources, legal NP gain data, preferred-primary-attacker and minimum-cost ranking. Results show the top distinct strategies or the bounded no-clear outcome, including party/CE assignments, cost, causal attackers, and action order. Mystic Code activation, Order Change, support servants, Event/Bond CE preferences, reliability trials, replay, Max Burst, and Manual/Sandbox remain deferred.

**2026-11-06 amendment (navigation):** the web app has three top-level areas, matching the approved wireframes in `design/`. **Servants** holds the collection: servants, Craft Essences, Mystic Codes and Master level. **Clear Optimizer** has two steps, 1 Quest and 2 Find composition. Find composition combines the former Preferences and Results stages and exposes the search's turn limit (1–6, with 3 as the 3T-farming choice). **Damage Calculator** is the Manual/Sandbox theorycrafting surface; it is designed but not yet built, and shows a placeholder. The styling is the neutral wireframe look and remains provisional until a `DESIGN.md` exists.

**2026-11-08 amendment (borrowed support):** support servants are no longer deferred. `optimizeQuest` takes `supportCandidates` (standard build from `buildBorrowedSupportCandidate`) and searches every party with no support and with each candidate in one slot, front line or bench. The support takes no CE, is excluded from Cost, and gets its own battle key (`supportBattleId`) so it can field alongside the owned copy of the same servant. Find composition starts from the default support list above, lets the user add or remove supports, and marks the support in results. Each support adds another full party search, so run time grows with the list.

**2026-11-08 amendment (search levels, project owner's direction):** the optimizer searches at a chosen level instead of always trying every combination, which took about 33 minutes for one 4-servant party once every skill became searchable. Level 1 "quick" (`searchLevel: "quick"`, needs a chosen attacker) fields only parties with that attacker in front, puts CEs on the attacker alone, aims single-ally skills at it and keeps a narrow beam: it found a 3-turn clear for the owner's Morgan / double Koyanskaya of Light / Oberon party in about 20 seconds. Level 3 "thorough" (the default) keeps the full combination search and belongs in a background Web Worker with progress and Cancel. Level 2 (attacker plus the whole owned roster) and the web wiring of levels are not built yet. By default every wave must fall to NPs, played before any face card, and the next NP must be charged without face-card refund or an Arts Chain; both are lifted by the opt-in `allowFaceCardFinishes`, since they depend on future dealt hands. Finalists show a clear rate from replays on freshly dealt hands and are ranked by it. The quick level searches at the minimum damage roll, so the clears it suggests hold on low rolls. The equipped Mystic Code's skills are part of the search (engine option `mysticCode`; the web app does not pass it yet).

## Product Principles

- **Real owned-account state over hypothetical max investment** — every owned-servant/CE number (skill level, NP level, Fou, bond, CE MLB) reflects what the user actually owns. The one exception is the explicitly disclosed standardized build used when recommending an ideal borrowed support.
- **Turn count is an outcome within a bounded search horizon** — Clear Quest finds the earliest clear up to the configured cap; 3T is a preset, not a global default.
- **Objectives are separate; mechanics are shared** — Clear Quest minimizes turns, Max Burst maximizes target damage, and Manual/Sandbox replays user choices through the same simulator.
- **Prefer one attacker, never force one blindly** — expand to multi-attacker strategies when a single-attacker line cannot clear reliably; reliability outranks attacker-count simplicity.
- **Community strategies emerge from simulation, not hardcoded patterns** — known lines (Arts loop, Buster loop, etc.) should fall out of simulate+search, used as heuristics to prune the search space, not as the only options.
- **Primary-source data only** — every formula/number traces to Atlas Academy's API/schema or the `fgo-docs` repo; unsourced mechanics are explicitly flagged, never silently assumed.
- **Failure is actionable** — when nothing clears, report the closest line and the smallest concrete upgrade that would make it clear, not just "not possible."
